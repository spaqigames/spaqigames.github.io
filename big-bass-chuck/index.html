Big Bass Chuck

Fishing · Simulation · Sports · Android

Lake Erie to the Andes. The fish are real. The stories are mostly true.

Chuck is a fisherman with a big mouth and an even bigger tackle box. Travel from his home in Akron, Ohio across more than 250 real places around the world, modelled from real maps, and catch everything that swims.

Real places

  • Parks and lakes all over Ohio, the Cleveland waterfront and the Lake Erie islands
  • Pittsburgh, New York, San Francisco, the Everglades, Loch Ness, the Andes and dozens of countries
  • Real shorelines, trails, bridges and skylines

Serious fishing, silly everything else

  • Dozens of real species with real facts, plus rare catches to brag about
  • Rods, lures, rowboats, airboats and your own Pond Builder
  • Wrestle a gator. Get sprayed by a skunk. Hunt for Sasquatch, Champ and Nessie.
  • Photo and selfie mode with your own album

Sleep sounds

  • Drift off to the lapping lake, a woodland creek, a cattail marsh, the ocean shore or loons calling at night
  • Gentle birds that grow quieter as you fall asleep, a breathing guide, and a timer from 15 minutes to all night
  • Keeps playing with the screen off

Plenty of jokes, and locals with plenty to say. Plays offline, with quality settings to suit your phone.

Open new spots with game cash or by watching ads, or buy the ad-free version to open them all. No accounts.
