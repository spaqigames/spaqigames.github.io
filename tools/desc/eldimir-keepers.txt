The last invitation to the Great Gleam's party was delivered a year late. Gerald was carrying it. Gerald is a mushroom. Mushrooms don't run.

Now the Umbra Profunda, who has waited since before the mountains to be invited to anything, has read the date on the card, and every shadow that ever sulked is marching. They have been told to start with the stew.

Eldimir: Keepers of the Light is a 3D tower defense game set in the world of Eldimir & Mossback. Build towers, walk your heroes into the fight, and keep the light burning from the crooked cottage to the very bottom of the world.

A FULL STORY
• 14 chapters across two parts, from the stew pot to the Deep
• Funny, gentle storytelling with Gerald, the Glowcaps and a very opinionated great-aunt
• A story with Christian faith themes: holy ground, a frozen chapel, prayer and song

8 TOWERS, EACH WITH A CHOICE
• Glowcap, Shy Bluebells, Bell of the Order, Stone Lantern, Potion Shelf, Golden Sapling, Crystal Spire and Gerald, who turns up eventually
• Each tower has three levels, then a choice between two final forms

TWO HEROES ON THE FIELD
• Tap to send Eldimir anywhere. He relights dimmed towers, spots hidden shades and casts Lux Divina
• Mossback is slow. He blocks, boops, leaves a slowing trail and sings the canticum fidei

6 BOSSES, REDEEMED AND NEVER DESTROYED
• Great-Aunt Glum, Mother Murk, the Umbra Minor, the Second Sentinel, the Last Yawn and the Umbra Profunda
• Each has a weak spot to work out, and the Field Guide will help

PLENTY TO COME BACK FOR
• Moonless Night: a hard mode with 3 more stars to earn in every chapter
• Gentle shadows: an easier setting for anyone who wants the story
• Stew Night: an endless mode
• 8 hats to unlock with your stars, documented in Hats: A History
