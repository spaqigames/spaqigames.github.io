Can you name the capital of every state? Not New York City, not Chicago, not Los Angeles. Capital Quest helps you learn them for good.

Every question lights up the place on a real map, so you learn where it is as well as its capital. After each answer you get a short fact, like why Juneau can't be reached by road or which Capitol has oil wells on its lawn.

GAME MODES
• Daily Challenge: 10 new questions every day. Keep your streak going!
• Classic Quest: all 50 states. Score well to unlock the U.S. Territories bonus round and the World Capitals super bonus.
• Find It on the Map: tap the state or country on the map.
• Flags: name the country, or pick the right flag from look-alikes.
• Nicknames & Birds: which state is the Buckeye State? What is Ohio's state bird?
• Beat the Clock: how many capitals can you get in 60 seconds?
• Endless: keep going until your three hearts run out.
• World Capitals: all 195 countries, one region at a time.

TRICKY ANSWERS
