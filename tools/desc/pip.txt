Pip is an apprentice wizard, three and a half apples tall. Pip has to get the Heart Acorn back from a very sulky mushroom king, cure a toad baron of catastrophic gas, and stop a moderately evil wizard from stinking up the forest to win an award.

And then somebody steals the moon.

A BIG LITTLE ADVENTURE
• Three parts and 14 chapters of dungeons, puzzles and boss fights
• Bogs, pirate ships, haunted manors, cloud kingdoms, jungles, glaciers and a robot factory
• A crab who thinks the moon is a pearl, a teething giant baby and a very tired vampire
• Dance-offs. There are dance-offs.

SPELLS FOR EVERY OCCASION
• Learn five spells, right up to the room-shaking Bass Drop
• Every boss has its own signature move and fair warning signs

MADE FOR PHONES
• Simple move stick and big spell buttons
• Game controllers work too
• Plays offline, and saves your progress on your phone
