Raise your very own butterfly, from a tiny egg to beautiful wings!

Petalwing is a gentle 3D pet game about one of nature's most amazing changes. Pick your butterfly, give it a name, and care for it through every stage of its real life cycle: egg, caterpillar, chrysalis and butterfly.

8 REAL BUTTERFLIES, 8 GARDENS
• Monarch in the Milkweed Meadow
• Black Swallowtail in the Herb Garden
• Eastern Tiger Swallowtail in the Woodland Glade
• Spicebush Swallowtail by the Creek & Marsh
• Gulf Fritillary on the Seaside Dunes
• Blue Morpho by the Rainforest River
• Glasswing in the Cloud Forest
• Painted Lady in the Desert Bloom

A CATERPILLAR TO LOOK AFTER
• Caterpillars are fussy eaters! Pick the one plant your caterpillar really eats, then watch the fresh leaf arrive and see it munch real bites out of the edge
• Tidy up, play together and let it nap
• Help it wriggle out of its old skin as it grows through 5 sizes
• Watch it hang, spin silk and become a chrysalis that slowly turns clear

A BUTTERFLY TO FLY WITH
• Help it pump up its new wings and let them dry in the sun
• Offer flower nectar, fruit or a sugar-water sponge
• Bask in sunbeams, then fly free to find nectar
• Lay eggs and raise the next generation for your album

6 MINI-GAMES
• Leafy Maze: climb 8 giant plants, spin silk bridges and sneak past ants, wasps, spiders and birds
• Wriggle Out: wiggle in time to shed your skin
• Guard Duty: gently shoo ants and wasps away from the chrysalis
• Dream Wings: put a wing pattern back together
• Nectar Quest: fly free through your garden and find nectar in surprising places
• Prism Wings: a magical flying adventure through 10 realms, with spells in your butterfly's own colours and 10 big bosses to set free

LEARN AS YOU PLAY
• Real butterfly facts at every stage: why monarchs taste yucky to birds, how swallowtail caterpillars pop out "stinky horns", and lots more
• Real bird songs and garden sounds, with soft, soothing music
• Optional gentle reminders when your butterfly needs you, never between 8pm and 8am

FOR KIDS AND FAMILIES
• No accounts, nothing to type except a name, and it plays offline
• Buying and links out of the game are behind a question for grown-ups
• Touch, keyboard and mouse, or a game controller. Works on phones, tablets and Chromebooks

Free with ads that are suitable for children, or remove them with one purchase. Optional: after a tumble in Prism Wings, watch an ad to keep flying from where you were.
