Sit quietly, listen, and find the birds.

Birdwatch is a calm 3D birdwatching game. Choose a place and a time of day, listen to nature, and zoom in on birds in fine detail as they land around you.

SIX PLACES, THREE TIMES OF DAY
• Woodland Trail, Backyard Garden, Lakeside Marsh, Prairie Meadow, Atlantic Seashore and Northern Pine Forest
• Dawn chorus, busy midday feeders, and owls and frogs at dusk

62 BIRDS OF THE NORTHEASTERN US
• Detailed 3D birds with their own feathers and colors
• Every species sings with a real recording of that species
• A field guide full of facts, and a list of the birds you've found

Recordings were made by naturalists and are shared under open licences. Everyone is credited in the app.

Free with ads, or remove them with one purchase. No accounts. Just birds.
