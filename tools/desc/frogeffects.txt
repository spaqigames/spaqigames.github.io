Have you ever wondered what a Bullfrog actually sounds like? Or a Red-eyed Tree Frog?

FrogEffects puts 52 species of frogs and toads in your hand as 3D models you can spin and explore, and every one calls with a real recording of its own species.

EXPLORE
• Spin each frog around and tap any part of it to see a fact
• Press the big button to hear its real call, and watch its vocal sac puff along
• Browse the full list of species

LEARN
• Where each species lives and how big it gets
• A quiz to test what you've learned: "Who's calling?"
• Stuck? Watch a short ad for a 50/50 that takes away two wrong frogs (three per quiz, free with Remove Ads)

NIGHT POND
• Sleep & relax: fall asleep beside a night-time pond or creek while real frogs call, fewer and fewer as you drift off, with a calming breathing guide
• Choose frogs from around the world or from one continent, and a timer from 15 minutes to all night
• Frog alarm: wake up to a frog chorus or your favourite frog, starting softly and growing louder

REAL SOUNDS
Recordings were made by naturalists and are shared under open licences. Everyone is credited in the app.

Free with ads, or remove them with one purchase. No accounts. Great for curious kids, teachers and frog people of all ages.
