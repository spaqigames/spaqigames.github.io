Hatch an egg. Raise a tadpole. Watch it grow back legs, then front legs, then lose its tail and become a frog. Your frog.

Lil' Ribbit is a 3D pet game for kids, with lots of burps, jokes and googly-eyed comedy.

LOOK AFTER YOUR PET
• Name your pet and feed, wash and play with it
• It grows over a few real days as you care for it
• Your pet never dies. It just gets a bit sad and misses you.
• Friendly reminders when your pet needs you, and none between 8pm and 8am

GROWN-UP FROG GAMES
• Pond Adventure: swim and dive around a big open world, find pals and finish quests
• Big Adventure: a sideways platform adventure with levels and bosses
• Hoppy Road, Fly Snack and Bubble Pop

Over 40 jokes, stickers to collect and hats to wear.

Free to play with ads, or remove them with one purchase. No accounts.
