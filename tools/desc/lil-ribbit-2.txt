Somebody has stolen all the giggles in the world, and the sneezing toad wizard Snivelwart has a lot of explaining to do.

Lil' Ribbit 2: The Great Giggle Heist is a 3D platform adventure. Run, jump and tongue-grab your way through a big, silly story with Flicker, a firefly who is afraid of the dark.

A HUGE ADVENTURE
• 10 worlds with 3 levels each, plus a different boss at the end of every world
• Rescue the tadpoles hidden in every level, including the secret ones
• Checkpoints, so you never lose too much progress
• Spend the flies you collect on 12 hats, from a Pirate Hat to a Royal Crown

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Adjust the button size, camera speed and sound
• Quality settings to suit your device

Free to play with ads, or remove them with one purchase. No accounts.
