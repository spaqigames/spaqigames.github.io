The gloom is spreading through the gardens, and only a butterfly can stop it.

Prism Wings is a magical 3D rail-flight shooter in the style of the classic dragon-riding arcade games, with bright, storybook colours. Fly a winding path of light through ten realms, aim your magic, dodge everything the gloom throws at you and blast the gloom beasts to sparkling pieces.

8 butterflies, 8 kinds of magic

  • Fly as a Monarch, Black Swallowtail, Tiger Swallowtail, Spicebush Swallowtail, Gulf Fritillary, Blue Morpho, Glasswing or Painted Lady
  • Every butterfly casts its spells in its own wing colours
  • Swoop, bank and dodge with a light, quick feel. Your butterfly really leans into every turn

Aim, lock on, cast

  • Hold to fire a stream of magic wherever you aim
  • Sweep your aim over several enemies to lock on, then let go to send homing darts at all of them. Take out lots in one volley for a big lock-on bonus
  • Fill your Glimmer and cast three big spells: Petal Storm, Rainbow Beam and Starfall
  • Turn your view to spot creatures coming from the sides and behind, and watch the radar

10 realms, 20 levels, 10 bosses

  • Dawn Meadow, Honey Hollow, Sunflower Sea, Lily Lake, Bullfrog Bog, Amber Woods, Crystal Caverns, The Rose Briars, Cloudtop Kingdom and the Moonlit Prism Garden
  • Every realm ends in a boss fight where you circle the boss and break its weak spots: Weaveria the Webmother, Captain Stingwell, Duchess Mantissa, Sir Dartwing, King Gulpus, Blusterjay, Ironhorn, the Thornqueen, Stormcrown Nimbus and Umbra, Queen of Gloom
  • Each boss has its own attacks, from real stingers, boulders and hailstones to silk webs, bubbles, flames and storms of stars, plus a signature spell you can see charging up
  • Stars for every level, and a gentle helping hand if a boss keeps beating you

Made for playing anywhere

  • An epic score of drums, strings, brass, choir and harp, made live in the game
  • Touch, keyboard and mouse, or an Xbox-style controller
  • On-screen controls can be hidden for Google Play Games on PC
  • Works on phones, tablets and Chromebooks, and plays offline with no accounts

Free with ads, or remove them with one purchase. Optional: after a fall, watch an ad to keep flying from right where you were.
