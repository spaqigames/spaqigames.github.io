Sit quietly, listen, and find the birds.

Songbird Safari is a calm 3D birdwatching game. Choose a place and a time of day, listen to nature, and zoom in on birds in fine detail as they land around you.

Six places, three times of day

  • Woodland Trail, Backyard Garden, Lakeside Marsh, Prairie Meadow, Atlantic Seashore and Northern Pine Forest
  • Dawn chorus, busy midday feeders, and owls and frogs at dusk

62 birds of the northeastern US

  • Detailed 3D birds with their own feathers and colors
  • Every species sings with a real recording of that species
  • A field guide full of facts, and a list of the birds you've found

Sleep & relax

  • Fall asleep to an evening in any of the six places: nature sounds with gentle birdsong that grows quieter as you drift off, then slowly fades away
  • Choose 15 minutes to all night, with a calming breathing guide on a dark, dimmed screen
  • Keeps playing with the screen off

Bird alarm

  • Wake up to real birdsong instead of a beep: a whole dawn chorus from any habitat, or your favourite bird
  • Starts softly and grows louder over a minute, with snooze right on the lock screen
  • Set different alarms for weekdays and weekends

Recordings were made by naturalists and are shared under open licences. Everyone is credited in the app.

Free with ads, or remove them with one purchase. No accounts. Just birds.
