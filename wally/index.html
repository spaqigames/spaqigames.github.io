Wally and the Wobblelands

Action · Adventure · Platformer · RPG · Android

Out now on Google Play
Get it on Google PlayMore on YouTube

Wally is a very small hero with a very big problem, and a wooden spoon to solve it with.

Wally and the Wobblelands is a side-scrolling action adventure in the classic style: explore a huge world, talk to everyone, buy better gear and beat bosses that each fight in their own ridiculous way.

A whole epic

  • 20 chapters and 20 bosses, every one with warning signs you can learn to read
  • Forests, deserts, custard seas, frozen peaks and a storm at the end of the world
  • Shops, inns, campfires, secrets and a cloud called Puff

Fight your way

  • Swing, stab, and bounce off monsters' heads with the downward stab
  • Fireball runs on mana: tap to fling one, or hold to charge a whopper
  • Bottomless Beans, the most powerful magic in the world. You will hear it.

Made for phones

  • Full-screen with a proper D-pad and buttons
  • Bluetooth and USB game controllers work too
  • Plays offline, and saves your progress on your phone

No accounts. Plays offline. Lots of comedy.
