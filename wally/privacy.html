Wally and the Wobblelands Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

In short: Wally and the Wobblelands keeps your game information on your device. It has no accounts and no analytics, and the developer has no server, so the developer never sees it. The free version shows ads from Google, described below.

What the app stores

The app saves your saved game and settings in its own storage on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this saved progress in your phone's backup to your Google account, so it comes back on a new phone. Google handles that backup under its own privacy policy, and the developer cannot see it.

Your age

The first time you open the free version, the app asks what year you were born. It uses the answer once, keeps only whether you are 13 or older, and never sends it to the developer. You can skip the question.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. If you skip the age question or are under 13, ads are not personalized and the app does not use the advertising ID. If you are 13 or older, Google may collect your internet address, the advertising ID and information about your device and how you use the ads, and ads may be personalized where you allow it. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

The one-time Remove Ads purchase is handled by Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details.

Reporting a problem

The app keeps a short list of its recent errors on your device. If you choose Report a Problem, your email app opens with a message to the developer containing that list, your phone model, your Android version and the app version. You can read or change it, and nothing is sent unless you press Send. The developer will then have your email address and only uses it to answer you.

What else leaves your device

Nothing. Your game progress and anything you type stay on your device.

Children

This app is suitable for children. The developer does not collect personal information from anyone. Anything typed into the app stays on the device. Buying is behind a question for grown-ups. Parents can erase everything by uninstalling the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if a later version collects something new, the new version will be posted at this address before that version is released.
