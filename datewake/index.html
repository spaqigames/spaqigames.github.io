DateWake

Most alarm clocks only understand days of the week. Life doesn't work that way.

DateWake rings at different times on different dates, whether it's a 4:45 flight on Friday, a changing shift pattern or trash day every other week. Set each one on a calendar, right next to your events, and let your regular alarm carry on as usual.

Alarms for the days you actually need them

  • Repeat on weekdays, pick exact dates on the calendar, or do both
  • Tap any day and set an alarm for that day
  • Skip one morning, or ring at a different time just once, without changing the alarm
  • Add several times to the same date: 6:00, 6:10, 6:20
  • Choose each alarm's sound: the phone's alarm sounds, or any song or sound file
  • A big alarm screen over the lock screen, with Snooze and Stop

Your calendar at a glance

  • See events from the calendars on your phone, including Google Calendar
  • Colored dots show which days are busy
  • Your contacts' birthdays appear automatically, with ages when the year is known
  • A home-screen widget with the time, your next alarm and what's coming up

A clock you can read across the room

  • A big, clear clock with the date, your next alarm and your next event
  • Turn your phone sideways on a stand for a bedside clock
  • Light and dark themes that follow your phone

Holidays and faith calendars

  • US holidays, including the observed day off
  • Add Christian, Jewish, Muslim, Hindu, Buddhist and Sikh holidays with a tap
  • A full US Catholic calendar: holy days, saints' days, fasting days and each day's Mass readings, with short stories of the saints
  • Dates are worked out on your phone, for any year

Private by design

DateWake has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet. Your calendar, contacts and alarms stay on your phone.

Works on phones, foldables and tablets.
