DateWake Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

In short: DateWake keeps everything on your phone. It has no internet access, no accounts, no ads and no analytics, so it cannot send your information anywhere, and the developer never sees it.

What the app reads, and why

  • Calendar (read only). To show the events from the calendars on your phone (such as Google Calendar) on the app's calendar and home-screen widget. The app does not change or delete your events. The "Event" button opens your calendar app so you can add one there.
  • Contacts (read only). To show your contacts' birthdays on the calendar. Only names and birthdays are used, and only on your phone.
  • Notifications, exact alarms and full-screen alerts. So your alarms ring on time, show over the lock screen and give you Stop and Snooze buttons.
  • Running after a restart, vibration and keeping the phone awake. So alarms are set again after your phone restarts, and can vibrate and keep ringing until you stop them.
  • A sound or song you choose. If you pick a sound file for an alarm, Android gives the app permission to play that one file. The app does not look through your other files.

You can turn any of these permissions off in your phone's settings. The app keeps working, but without, for example, your calendar events or birthdays.

What the app stores

Your alarms and settings (such as which holidays to show) are saved in the app's own storage on your phone. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the app's alarms and settings in your phone's backup to your Google account, so they come back on a new phone. Google handles that backup under its own privacy policy; the developer cannot see it.

Holiday dates, saints' days and Mass reading references are worked out on your phone or built into the app. Nothing is downloaded.

What the app shares

Nothing. The app does not collect, send, sell or share any personal information with the developer or anyone else. It contains no advertising, tracking or analytics code.

If you tap Read in full on a day's Mass readings, your web browser opens that page on the US bishops' website (bible.usccb.org). That website is not part of the app and has its own privacy policy. The app itself has no internet access.

Children

The app is not aimed at children, and it collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
