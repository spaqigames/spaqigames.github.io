Every dog needs a walk, and so do we.

Walkies is a dog ownership game designed with older adults in mind. Pick from 261 breeds, raise your pup from a tiny bundle to a grown dog over about 30 days of good care, and take it out on real walks.

Raise your pup

  • Feed, water, pet, train and clean up after your dog
  • Learn about your breed with facts about all 261
  • Reminders come as real dog sounds: a bark for dinner, a whine for potty, a scratch at the door for a walk

Walk for real

  • Your real steps count, like a fitness tracker
  • A live map shows sniff spots to visit on your walk
  • Your dog does what dogs do along the way

Meet people

  • See when other Walkies players are nearby on a walk, with first names only
  • Keep a journal of the dogs you've met
  • Invite a friend from your phone

Easy to use

  • Big buttons and a font designed for easy reading
  • It can read things aloud

Walkies is a game, not medical advice. Check with your doctor before starting a new exercise routine.
