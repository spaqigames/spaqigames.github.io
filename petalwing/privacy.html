Petalwing Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

In short: Petalwing: My Butterfly Garden keeps your game information on your device. It has no accounts and no analytics, and the developer has no server, so the developer never sees it. The free version shows ads from Google that are suitable for children and never personalized, described below.

What the app asks for, and why

  • Notifications: optional reminders when your butterfly needs you (never between 8pm and 8am). They are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent from a server. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or your phone's settings.
  • Vibration: for little buzzes during play.

What the app stores

The app saves your butterfly's name, kind and care, your garden, your petals and hats, your mini-game progress and your settings in its own storage on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this saved progress in your phone's backup to your Google account, so it comes back on a new phone. Google handles that backup under its own privacy policy, and the developer cannot see it.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: sometimes a full-screen ad after a mini-game or between flight levels, and a video ad a player can choose to watch to keep flying after a fall. Every ad request is marked as directed to children, so ads are not personalized, are rated for general audiences, and the app does not use the advertising ID. Google may still receive your internet address and basic device information to show the ad. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

The one-time Remove Ads purchase is handled by Google Play and is behind a question for grown-ups. The developer never sees your payment details.

Reporting a problem

The app keeps a short list of its recent errors on your device. If you choose Report a Problem, your email app opens with a message to the developer containing that list, your phone model, your Android version and the app version. You can read or change it, and nothing is sent unless you press Send. The developer will then have your email address and only uses it to answer you.

What else leaves your device

Nothing. Your butterfly, garden and anything you type stay on your device.

Children

This app is made for children. The developer does not collect personal information from anyone. Anything typed into the app, like your butterfly's name, stays on the device. Buying, emails and links out of the app are behind a question for grown-ups. Parents can erase everything by uninstalling the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if a later version collects something new, the new version will be posted at this address before that version is released.
