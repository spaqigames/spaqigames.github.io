Welcome to Nonna Rosa's kitchen! Put on your apron, little chef. Today you cook a real meal... and then the meal fights back.

Every time dinner is ready, the grumpy villain Il Gran Formaggio (a very large, very proud wheel of Parmesan) pops up on the counter and sprinkles his jar of Pepe Pazzo, the crazy pepper. The food comes alive! Grab your spatula, bonk the ingredients gently, and they hop back into the Time-Out Pot to calm down.

Cook 20 real recipes

  • Pancakes, scrambled eggs, French toast, spaghetti and meatballs, garlic bread, tomato soup, cookies, blueberry muffins, tacos, salad, pizza, mac and cheese, fried rice, smoothies, apple crumble, chicken noodle soup, burgers, lasagna, brownies and a birthday cake
  • Every step is a mini-game: crack eggs, whisk, flip pancakes, chop, pour to the line, roll dough, blend without popping the lid, sprinkle toppings, bake until golden
  • Watch out for socks, rubber ducks and gummy worms. They do NOT go in the bowl!
  • Every meal unlocks its real recipe card, to cook at home with a grown-up

Then fight your food

  • Swat, jump on heads, throw cream pies, roll out of the way and spin the Whisk Whirlwind
  • Hopping eggs, slippery butter, sneezing flour bags, rolling meatballs, stinky garlic and a whole army of peas
  • 20 silly bosses: Sir Flapjack Stackington, Count Tomatula, Captain Crumbles, the Noodle Kraken, Mac-zilla, Granny Smash, the Fudge Judge and more
  • A happy ending: nobody ever remembered the Big Cheese's birthday...

For kids of all ages

  • Funny, gentle cartoon battles. Beaten food just gets dizzy and goes back in the pot
  • Little Chef mode: more hearts and gentler bosses
  • Make your own chef and win 21 silly hats
  • Classic Italian music played by a real-sounding band: Funiculì Funiculà, Santa Lucia, Torna a Surriento, a tarantella and the William Tell galop
  • Touch, keyboard and mouse, or a game controller. Works on phones, tablets and Chromebooks
  • Plays offline, with no accounts

Free with ads, or remove them with one purchase. Optional: after losing a fight, watch an ad to try again with 2 extra hearts.
