Eldimir & Mossback

Adventure · Action · Android

Early access on Google Play very soon
Get notified on YouTube

Eldimir is the old Wizard of the Glimmerwood. Mossback is his snail. Mossback is the size of a dog, acts like one, and goes everywhere with him.

When shadow falls over the enchanted forest, the two of them set out to bring back the light in a big, funny, magical 3D adventure.

A story worth telling

  • 14 chapters in two parts, from a crooked cottage to a frozen summit
  • A full Storybook to read from the title screen
  • Glowcaps, sulky Glumlings and Gerald, who is always late
  • A gentle story with Christian faith themes: Eldimir and Mossback pray at an altar, and a chapel's bells ring in the dawn

Five bosses, five ways to win

  • Each boss is beaten in its own way
  • Bosses are redeemed rather than destroyed

A very good snail

  • Mossback follows you and helps: boop shy flowers, pray, and sing when the song bar is full
  • Find 27 hidden Twinklings
  • Adjust the button size, camera and graphics to suit your phone

Free with ads, or remove them with one purchase. No accounts.
